    mechthild User Documentation

    Welcome to mechthild, the HPC-Cluster of the MPI Magdeburg.

    The system is named after Mechthild of Magdeburg, one of the famous persons in Magdeburg in the medieval age.

    Organisational Stuff

    To use the cluster you need to sign the current user agreement which can be found here: UserForm.pdf. This agreement must also be signed off by the head of your research group. Once both signatures are in, bring the form to the IT and to Marc Schütz (S3.04) or Martin Köhler (S3.20).

    The following communication channels exist:

    • The central support mail is hpc@mpi-magdeburg.mpg.de
    • The mailing list hpc-user@mpi-magdeburg.mpg.de is used to provide latest information to the users such as planed downtimes and new software.

    Important: There is no backup running on mechthild's filesystem.

    Using mechthild

    • Quickstart - This will explain you the first steps on mechthild and setting up the environment. This is a good point to start.
    • JupyterHub - mechthild also supports working with JupyterHub interactively from your browser.

    • CompileOwnSoftware - A small howto to compile your own software on mechthild.
    • Jobs - Details about submitting jobs on mechthild.
    • Partitions - Details about the available queues.
    • Hardware - Hardware details.
    • ResourceQuotas - Resource limits, like disk quota and maximum number of usable nodes.
